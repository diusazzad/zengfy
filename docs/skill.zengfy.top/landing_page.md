# Zengfy Skill (`skill.zengfy.top`) — Product & Landing Page Strategy

## Executive Summary
**Zengfy Skill** is the sovereign real-world skill education, project-based learning, and instructor LMS ERP platform integrated directly with **Zengfy Social**. Unlike traditional EdTech platforms that sell expensive video courses for profit without practical value, Zengfy Skill empowers SSC/HSC graduates and young learners with affordable, job-ready skills (Spoken English, Practical Computer & IT, Real-World Web & Software Projects) starting from **৳১২০০**. It also provides instructors and skill academies with an automated Learning Management System (LMS ERP) monetized through a fair software fee plus percentage revenue share.

---

## 1. Key Value Propositions & Core Vision

### Solving Bangladesh's Skill Education Gap
- **The Problem**: Expensive pre-recorded video courses costing ৳5,000–৳20,000 without mentorship, practical projects, or real-world application.
- **The Zengfy Skill Solution**:
  - Practical, project-focused bootcamps starting at **৳১২০০**.
  - Focus on SSC/HSC graduates needing Spoken English, Practical Computer & IT, and Web/Coding skills.
  - Live mentor Q&A, hands-on assignment reviews, and verified skill certificates.

---

## 2. Key Personas & Value Delivery

### Persona A: SSC/HSC Graduates & Skill Seekers (শিক্ষার্থী ও স্কিল ট্রেইনি)
- Real-world project bootcamps starting at **৳১২০০**.
- Lifetime course access, live mentor Q&A, and practical portfolio projects.

### Persona B: Instructors, Mentors & Academies (মেন্টর ও ইনস্ট্রাক্টর)
- Complete **LMS ERP Platform** to host live batches, manage student assignments, grade projects, and disburse digital certificates.
- **Monetization**: **৳৫০০ / মাস** software subscription + low percentage platform revenue share on course sales.

---

## 3. Core Technical & ERP Capabilities

1. **Real-World Project Curriculum (বাস্তবমুখী প্রজেক্ট কারিকুলাম)**:
   - Hands-on exercises for Spoken English, Applied Computer & IT, and Web Development.
2. **Instructor LMS ERP Portal (মেন্টর ও একাডেমি ইআরপি)**:
   - Live batch scheduling, student attendance, assignment submission tracking, and automated revenue share payouts.
3. **Verified Skill Certification (ভেরিফাইড স্কিল সার্টিফিকেট)**:
   - NID-authenticated digital skill certificates verifiable by employers.

---

## 4. Subscription & Pricing

- **Learners / Course Seekers**: **৳১২০০ থেকে শুরু** (লাইফটাইম কোর্স এক্সেস, প্রজেক্ট ও মেন্টর সহায়তা).
- **Instructors & Academies**: **৳৫০০ / মাস + পার্সেন্টেজ শেয়ার** (অল-ইন-ওয়ান LMS ERP, লাইভ ব্যাচ ও ডোমেইন কানেক্ট).
