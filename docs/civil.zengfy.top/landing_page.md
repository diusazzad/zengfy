# Zengfy Civil (`civil.zengfy.top`) — Product & Landing Page Strategy

## Executive Summary
**Zengfy Civil** is the sovereign civil engineering, building construction ERP, and verified contractor portal designed for civil engineers, architectural consultants, construction firms, and homeowners in Bangladesh. It features automated site raw material tracking (rod, cement, sand), contractor worker daily wages, **AI structural & soil blueprint analysis**, and a paid directory for homeowners searching for verified engineers and floor plan designs.

---

## 1. Key Value Propositions & Target Personas

### Persona A: Engineering Firms & Architectural Consultants (ইঞ্জিনিয়ারিং ফার্ম ও স্থপতি)
- **Pain Point**: Managing multiple site projects simultaneously, tracking raw material procurement, worker daily wages, and structural blueprint revisions.
- **Zengfy Civil Solution**:
  - Centralized site ERP for tracking rod, cement, brick, and sand inventory.
  - Subcontractor wage disbursements, milestone billing, and cloud blueprint vault.

### Persona B: Building Contractors & Site Managers (কন্ট্রাক্টর ও সাইট ম্যানেজার)
- **Pain Point**: Discrepancies in daily site expense logs, material theft, and labor attendance.
- **Zengfy Civil Solution**:
  - Real-time mobile log entries for site receipts, material delivery verification, and labor headcount.

### Persona C: Homeowners & Property Developers (সোশ্যাল ব্যবহারকারী ও বাড়ি নির্মাণকারী)
- **Pain Point**: Difficulty finding verified civil engineers, reliable building contractors, or authentic floor plans without overpaying.
- **Zengfy Civil Solution**:
  - Search verified engineering firms, structural engineers, and architectural blueprints with verified credentials (**৳১০০ / সার্চ কানেক্ট**).

---

## 2. Core Security & Technical Capabilities

1. **Civil ERP & Material Inventory Ledger (সাইট ইআরপি ও কাঁচামাল হিসাব)**:
   - Real-time tracking of raw material purchases (rod tonnage, cement bags, sand truckloads), contractor worker daily wages, and project milestone billing.
2. **AI Structural & Soil Advisor (AI সাইট ও ম্যাপ এনালাইসিস)**:
   - Smart AI evaluating building floor plans, soil test parameters, material cost forecasting, and structural load compliance.
3. **Paid Verified Engineer & Blueprint Search (পেইড কনসালট্যান্ট সার্চ)**:
   - Homeowners pay a nominal fee to search verified civil engineers, vetted contractors, and architectural house floor plans.

---

## 3. Subscription & Pricing

- **Homeowners / Property Builders**: **৳১০০ / সার্চ কানেক্ট** (ভেরিফাইড ইঞ্জিনিয়ার ও কনস্ট্রাক্টর ফাইল এক্সেস).
- **Engineering & Construction Firms**: **৳৮০০ / মাস** (অল-ইন-ওয়ান সাইট ইআরপি + AI ম্যাপ এনালাইসিস + ব্লুপ্রিন্ট ভল্ট).
