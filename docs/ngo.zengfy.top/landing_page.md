# Zengfy NGO (`ngo.zengfy.top`) — Product & Landing Page Strategy

## Executive Summary
**Zengfy NGO** is the sovereign transparent donation, emergency relief, and verified charity crowdfunding platform integrated directly with **Zengfy Social**. Donors can contribute to verified causes with 100% transparency and live funding counters. Campaign organizers, NGOs, and emergency relief teams can upload mandatory verification documents (NID, medical/disaster proof) and pay a nominal fee to launch featured campaigns and live stream fundraising sessions to millions of social users.

---

## 1. Key Value Propositions & Target Personas

### Persona A: Social Donors & Citizens (দাতা ও সাধারণ নাগরিক)
- **Pain Point**: Lack of transparency in charitable donations, fear of fake fundraisers on social media, unknown distribution status.
- **Zengfy NGO Solution**:
  - 100% verified authentic campaigns with mandatory document proof check.
  - Real-time live donation ticker showing exact raised amounts and transparent relief distribution ledgers.
  - **Fee Model**: **100% Free** for donors (0% platform charge).

### Persona B: NGOs, Relief Teams & Organizers (এনজিও ও প্রচারকারী)
- **Pain Point**: Difficulty reaching real donors without huge marketing budgets, fraud prevention barriers.
- **Zengfy NGO Solution**:
  - 1-click campaign submission with document verification (NID, hospital/disaster proof).
  - Ability to stream live fundraising updates directly to Zengfy Social users with 1-click bKash/Nagad/ZengPay donation buttons.
  - **Fee Model**: **৳৩০০** per verified campaign promotion & live stream session.

---

## 2. Core Security & Verification Architecture

1. **Document & Proof Authentication (সত্যায়িত প্রুফ ভেরিফিকেশন)**:
   - Organizers must submit NID and medical/disaster proof documents verified by the platform before going live.
2. **Real-Time Live Fund Counter (লাইভ ডোনেশন হিসাব)**:
   - Real-time funding progress bar, donor ledgers, and automated digital receipts.
3. **Social Live Stream Broadcast (সোশ্যাল লাইভ সেশন)**:
   - Verified campaign leaders host live updates on Zengfy Social with built-in instant donation buttons.

---

## 3. Subscription & Pricing

- **Donors**: **100% Free** (0% platform fee).
- **NGOs & Campaign Creators**: **৳৩০০ / ক্যাম্পেইন** (ভেরিফাইড প্রুফ চেক, সোশ্যাল লাইভ সেশন ও ফিচার্ড প্রচার প্যাক).
