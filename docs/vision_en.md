# 🚀 [zengfy.top](https://zengfy.top) (Main Gateway)

**Problem:**
We live in an age of technology and smartphones where social media consumption has become an addictive habit. This has led to several fundamental issues:
*   Instead of morning walks or real social interactions, people seek dopamine through endless scrolling.
*   The ability to set and focus on long-term goals is diminishing.
*   Lack of verified accounts (Fake Accounts) on most social platforms creates high security risks.
*   There is no effective way to immediately alert one's local community or family during physical danger or emergencies.

**Solution:**
Zengfy aims to solve these problems by promoting a healthy lifestyle and "Real Safety":
*   **Routine & Fitness:** The app will feature fitness tracking and tips alongside news feed alerts. It will also track app usage time to prevent addiction.
*   **Secure Community:** A dedicated section for friends and close circles (with likes/comments) that is completely safe and free from fake profiles.
*   **Verified Profiles:** Accounts will be verified using NID or institutional credentials to earn a 'Verify Badge,' ensuring a secure environment for youth.
*   **Emergency Alert (Unique Feature):** A button shortcut that activates an alert within the local community or defined circle, even without an active internet connection (via physical button combinations). This is the project's primary vision.
