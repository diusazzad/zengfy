# Zengfy Rent (`rent.zengfy.top`) — Product & Landing Page Strategy

## Executive Summary
**Zengfy Rent** is the sovereign digital property & rental vacancy ecosystem for Bangladesh. Integrated directly with **Zengfy Social**, it allows tenants to find verified available homes using an interactive **5 km / 10 km radius map search** without middleman brokers or fraudulent fake posts. Landlords can list vacant apartments with mandatory 100% verified ownership & security checks.

---

## 1. Key Value Propositions & Target Personas

### Persona A: Rent Seekers / Tenants (বাসা অনুসন্ধানকারী)
- **Pain Point**: Fake rental posts on Facebook groups, exorbitant broker fees (half to one month's rent), wasting days travelling across neighborhoods.
- **Zengfy Rent Solution**:
  - Interactive 5km / 10km radius circle map search based on current live location or pinned area.
  - 100% broker-free direct contact release with verified house owner.
  - **Fee Structure**: **৳৫০** per radius location search query & verified contact release.

### Persona B: House Owners & Landlords (বাড়িওয়ালা / প্রপার্টি ওনার)
- **Pain Point**: Vacant flats sitting empty for months, unauthorized sub-tenants, hassle of manually collecting monthly rent and issuing receipts.
- **Zengfy Rent Solution**:
  - 1-click vacancy listing broadcasted instantly to millions of nearby Zengfy Social users.
  - Verified landlord profile badge & automated digital rent collection via bKash/Nagad with digital receipt generation.
  - **Fee Structure**: **৳১৫০** per active vacancy listing post.

---

## 2. Security & Trust Architecture

1. **Smart Verified Property ID (স্মার্ট ভেরিফাইড প্রপার্টি আইডি)**:
   - Every listed rental unit receives a unique QR & encrypted property ID verified against owner credentials.
2. **Broker-Free Guarantee (ব্রোকারমুক্ত সরাসরি মালিক কানেক্ট)**:
   - Direct encrypted chat and phone connect between verified tenant and landlord.
3. **Automated Digital Rent Receipt (ডিজিটাল ভাড়া রসিদ)**:
   - Rent payment integration with instant bKash/Nagad transaction matching and automated SMS/App receipt.
